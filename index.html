How a project runs

Survey to commissioning

The same four-stage sequence on every project, regardless of building size.

01
Site Survey
We measure the building as-built — loads, floor plates, plant space and existing services — before any drawing is produced.
02
System Design
HVAC, ducting, piping and insulation are modelled together, with load calculations and layouts issued for approval.
03
Fabrication & Install
Ductwork and pipework are fabricated off-site where possible and installed in sequence with other trades.
04
Commissioning & Handover
Every zone is balanced, pressure-tested and measured against design airflow before handover documentation is issued.
